# Voice Foundation v1 Closure — 2026-08-15

The ADR-0041–0045 interface and denial backbone is complete as a private, inert Core foundation. It is not production ready and is not an independent attestation of consent, owner identity, revocation, anti-impersonation, disclosure, watermark, provenance, erasure, restore-negative testing, audit durability or lawful retention.

Terminal contract version: `sinbad-voice-terminal-denial/1-v1`.

Implemented scope: exact consent/disclosure/use-intent candidates; revocation/quarantine/emergency-stop targets; eight-class draft deletion manifest and unverified evidence candidates; honest `PARTIAL_DELETION`; content-minimized local incident hash-chain candidates; and terminal denial of every declared voice capability.

Open activation blockers include authenticated owner identity and consent, durable consent/revocation storage, purpose/time/language/user enforcement, sandbox and anti-impersonation assurance, disclosure/watermark and provenance custody, provider/replica/cache/backup inventory, destructive-operation authorization, cryptographic erasure, key destruction, independent restore-negative testing, authenticated actor/trusted time, signatures, append-only audit, non-reconstruction review, privacy/legal approval and retention authorization.

No evidence accumulation in v1 can unlock capture, voiceprint creation, cloning, synthesis, playback, identity/payment/legal/emergency-call use, incident-report retention, deletion completion, emergency-revocation restore/veto or activation. A positive path requires a new contract version, ADR, threat/privacy review, independent assurance and explicit authorization. “Closure” means only that the private inert interface and terminal-denial scope is regression-locked.
